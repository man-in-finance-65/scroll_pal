# Launch plan: people who already care about food and training

Vicente's idea (Oct 20): launch through run clubs, nutritionists and personal trainers, starting with Anytime Fitness. They'll help us get the best plans.

## Why this fits the build
- **Nutritionists / registered dietitians:** BUILD_PLAN §5 needs one registered dietitian to review `config/nutrition.ts`, the screening rules and `evidence/pack.json` **before any tester uses Pal**. A nutritionist partner covers that check and becomes a credible voice in the demo.
- **Personal trainers:** their clients already want meal plans and already save food reels. A trainer can bring 3 to 5 testers in one conversation.
- **Run clubs:** one weekly run gives you 20 to 50 adults in one place, and many are trying to lose weight. Good for Gate G (10 testers by Saturday) and for the live "Yes, I want Pal" counter (Gate F).

## What to bring
- The phone with Pal on it (Gate A build), and a QR code linking to the tester sign-up.
- One sentence: "Pal turns the food reels you save into a week of meals that fits your schedule, and a shopping list."
- The safety line: "Pal is for adults, doesn't give medical advice, and sends anyone with a medical condition to a doctor or dietitian."

## Rules that apply
- Testers must be 18+ and go through the in-app screening.
- Don't collect health details on paper or in a form. The app stores only eligible yes/no.
- Only claim a trainer or gym partnership in the Devpost if they actually agreed to it.
